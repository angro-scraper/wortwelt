WortWelt Premium can be reviewed in the protected Parents area:

1. On the home screen, tap "Für Eltern".
2. Enter 7 as the answer to 4 + 3.
3. Tap "Elternbereich öffnen".
4. The "WortWelt Premium" card shows the monthly subscription, the 7-day free trial, purchase and restore controls.

The first three stories and the first three fairy tales remain free. Further library entries require WortWelt Premium. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking or microphone access. Learning progress stays locally on the device. Purchases and subscription management use StoreKit through the App Store.
